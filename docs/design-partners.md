# Design partners (P0-5): template

Keep the real list (names, contacts, notes) in a private document. This template is the public shape of the programme.

## Profile

8–10 targets across fintech/NBFC (KYC, loan documents), health (ABHA, lab reports), legal, and BPO/support. Each should already send customer text to an LLM, or be blocked from doing so by privacy concerns.

## Offer

- Free shadow-mode pilot: gateway or LiteLLM guardrail in observe-only mode for 2–4 weeks.
- Weekly 30-minute findings review (types and counts only; we never see their data).
- Input into sector packs and the console roadmap; pricing discovery at the end.

## Success signals

- A "we didn't know that was happening" finding in week 1 of shadow mode.
- Moves from shadow to enforce within the pilot.
- Asks about the console, SSO or evidence exports (Phase 3–4 pull).
