# Brand guidelines

| | |
|---|---|
| Company | **Safearly** (`safearly.com`) |
| Product | **Saaf** (साफ़, "clean"). On first mention write "Saaf by Safearly". |
| Tagline | *Clean prompts. Caught early.* |
| One-liner | India-first PII masking and restore for LLM traffic. |

## The mark: "the sun behind the bar"

A saffron sun, partly hidden behind a green rounded bar.

- **Sun = early.** It sits mostly above the bar, so it reads as rising.
- **Bar = safe.** It is the redaction mask Saaf puts over a value. The sun is still recognisable behind it, the way a masked prompt is still useful to the model.
- **A sliver of sun below the bar** shows it is *behind* the mask rather than resting on it. This is what makes the silhouette unique: not a hat, not a sunset icon.
- Two shapes, two flat colours, no container. It reads at 16px and can be drawn from memory.

Rejected alternatives, kept for the record: a sun resting on a bar (read as a hat, then as a burger), a striped retro sun (a common motif, and its stripes vanish below 32px), and a sunrise with rays (a generic weather icon).

**Saaf product mark:** brackets around the same pill, `[▬]`, a token. It shares its DNA with the company mark.

Rules:
- Keep clear space of at least one bar-height around the mark.
- Never recolour, rotate, outline, add a shadow or put the mark in a container. App icons are the only exception: they use a plain white tile.
- The wordmark is lowercase "safearly", in medium weight.

## Layout and type

The style is minimal and product-led: white space, centred section headings, borderless tinted cards with 24px radius, pill buttons, and one primary action per view. Type is the system UI stack (Google Sans or Segoe UI where present) at medium weight with tight tracking, and no web fonts, so the site makes no third-party requests.

## Voice

- Plain, specific, honest. Lead with what Saaf does for the reader.
- Always say what Saaf **doesn't** catch, next to what it does.
- Never claim compliance. Write "evidence and safeguards for your DPDP work". CI enforces this (`scripts/check_wording.py`).
