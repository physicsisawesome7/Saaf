## The mark: "two layers"

Two offset pills that form an abstract S: a violet pill above and to the right, a black pill below and to the left.

- **Two layers = the product.** Safearly sits between an application and its models: the violet pill is the request guarded on the way in, the black one the reply on the way out.
- **The offset = speed.** The layers step forward rather than stacking square, so the mark reads as motion, not a wall.
- **The S** is there for Safearly without spelling it out.
- Two shapes, two flat colours, no container. It reads at 16px and can be drawn from memory. On dark backgrounds the black pill turns white.

Earlier marks, kept for the record: a sun partly hidden behind a bar (saffron and green until Oct 2026, then violet and black), retired for the two-layers mark when Safearly repositioned around inference, guardrails and agents.

**Saaf product mark:** brackets around the same pill, `[▬]`, a token. It shares its DNA with the company mark.

Rules:
- Keep clear space of at least one bar-height around the mark.
- Never recolour, rotate, outline, add a shadow or put the mark in a container. App icons are the only exception: they use a plain white tile.
- The wordmark is lowercase "safearly", in medium weight.

## Layout and type

The style is an editorial research-lab look: white space, left-aligned section heads with a mono uppercase eyebrow in violet, 1px-bordered cards with 16px radius, 10px-radius buttons (black primary, violet accent, white outline), and mono for metadata (dates, repo paths, numbers). Type is Inter where installed, falling back to the system UI stack, with no web fonts, so the site makes no third-party requests. Design source of truth: `.superdesign/design-system.md` and `apps/website/assets/styles.css` + `lab.css`.

## Voice

- Plain, specific, honest. Lead with what Saaf does for the reader.
- Always say what Saaf **doesn't** catch, next to what it does.
- Never claim compliance. Write "evidence and safeguards for your DPDP work". CI enforces this (`scripts/check_wording.py`).
