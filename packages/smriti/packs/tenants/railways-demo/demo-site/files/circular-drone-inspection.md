# Use of drones for bridge inspection

Circular No. NR/ENG/2026/03 dated 02.04.2026. Synthetic document for the Smriti demo.

Divisions may use drones for the visual inspection of the superstructure of important and major bridges, in addition to and not in place of the annual inspection by the bridge inspector.

Drone flights over the track require a traffic block or a lookout arrangement as for any work on the track, and footage shall be kept for three years.
