# Correction Slip No. 7 to the General Conditions of Contract, April 2022

Dated 25.09.2024. Synthetic document for the Smriti demo.

In partial modification of the General Conditions of Contract, April 2022, the Board has decided that Clause 16.1 shall be read as:

"The successful bidder shall submit a Performance Guarantee of 5% of the contract value within 30 days from the date of issue of the Letter of Acceptance."

This correction slip takes effect from the date of issue and applies to all tenders opened on or after that date.
