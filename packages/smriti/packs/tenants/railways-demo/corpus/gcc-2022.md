# General Conditions of Contract, April 2022 (synthetic demo edition)

This is a synthetic document written for the Smriti demo. Clause numbers follow a familiar layout; the wording and figures are invented.

## Part I: General

### 7. Bid Security

7.1 Every bidder shall furnish a bid security of 2% of the estimated value of the work, subject to a maximum of Rs 1 crore, or a bid security declaration where the tender permits it.

7.2 The bid security of unsuccessful bidders shall be released within 30 days of the award of contract.

### 16. Performance Guarantee

16.1 The successful bidder shall submit a Performance Guarantee of 5% of the contract value within 21 days from the date of issue of the Letter of Acceptance.

16.2 If the contractor fails to submit the Performance Guarantee within the period stated in Clause 16.1, a late fee of 0.1% of the contract value per day of delay shall be charged for up to 7 days, beyond which the Letter of Acceptance shall be cancelled and the bid security forfeited.

16.3 The Performance Guarantee shall remain valid until 60 days after the date of completion of the work.

### 17. Extension of Time

17.1 If the work is delayed by reasons beyond the contractor's control, the contractor may apply for an extension of time within 14 days of the hindrance arising.

17.2 Extension granted for reasons attributable to the contractor shall attract liquidated damages under Clause 46.

## Part II: Execution

### 46. Liquidated Damages

46.1 For delay attributable to the contractor, liquidated damages shall be recovered at 0.5% of the contract value per week of delay, subject to a maximum of 10% of the contract value.

46.2 Liquidated damages are a genuine pre-estimate of loss and are not a penalty.

### 62. Termination for Default

62.1 The contract may be terminated after a 7-day notice if the contractor abandons the work or fails to make progress for 28 days.

### 63. Settlement of Disputes

63.1 Disputes up to Rs 5 crore shall be referred to a sole arbitrator; disputes above Rs 5 crore to a panel of three arbitrators.
