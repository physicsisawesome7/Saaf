# Standard operating procedure: issue of Letter of Acceptance

Synthetic document for the Smriti demo.

1. Obtain the tender committee recommendation and the accepting authority's approval.
2. Confirm that the accepting authority has the power to accept the value under the powers of acceptance letter in force.
3. Obtain prior vetting by Associate Finance where required.
4. Issue the Letter of Acceptance within the validity of the offer.
5. Ask the contractor to submit the performance guarantee within 21 days as per Clause 16.1 of GCC.
6. Track receipt of the performance guarantee and apply the late fee under Clause 16.2 of GCC if delayed.
