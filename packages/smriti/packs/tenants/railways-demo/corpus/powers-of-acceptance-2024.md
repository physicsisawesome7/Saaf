# Powers of acceptance of works tenders

Letter No. BRD/2024/W1/POA dated 13.09.2024. Synthetic document for the Smriti demo.

In supersession of Board's letter No. BRD/2019/W1/POA dated 01.07.2019, the revised powers of acceptance of works tenders are given below. A Letter of Acceptance shall not be issued without prior vetting by Associate Finance where the value exceeds the limit in the third column.

| Designation | Maximum value (Rs crore) | Finance vetting required above (Rs crore) |
|---|---|---|
| Senior Divisional Engineer | 10 | 5 |
| Divisional Railway Manager | 50 | 10 |
| Chief Engineer (Construction) | 100 | 25 |
| Principal Chief Engineer | 500 | 25 |
| General Manager | Full powers | 25 |

Thresholds change from time to time. Officers shall always refer to the letter in force on the date of acceptance.
