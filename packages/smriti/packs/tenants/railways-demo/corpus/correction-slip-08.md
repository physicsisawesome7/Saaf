# Correction Slip No. 8 to the General Conditions of Contract, April 2022

Dated 20.12.2024. Synthetic document for the Smriti demo.

In partial modification of the General Conditions of Contract, April 2022, Clause 46.1 shall be read as:

"For delay attributable to the contractor, liquidated damages shall be recovered at 0.5% of the contract value per week of delay, subject to a maximum of 5% of the contract value."

The reduced ceiling applies to contracts whose Letter of Acceptance is issued on or after 20.12.2024.
