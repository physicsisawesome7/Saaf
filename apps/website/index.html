The problem

Everything between a model and production.

Scaling inference

Serving cost and latency decide what ships. We work on inference optimization: more tokens per GPU, lower tail latency, and numbers you can reproduce on your own workload.

Fast, secure guardrails

A guardrail that adds a second to every request gets switched off. Ours run on CPU in about a millisecond, so they can stay on for every request, in the stream.

Secure agents and skills

Agents act through tools, and that is where data leaves. We build skills that declare what they need, run with least privilege, and get checked before they act.

Saaf

LiveApache-2.0

Fast guardrails for LLM traffic. Masks personal data before it reaches a model and puts the real values back in the reply, even mid-stream. 22 identifier types with checksums, zero dependencies, in Python, TypeScript and as a gateway.

Saaf Guard

In research

A firewall for agent tool calls. Deterministic policy on every call: allow-lists, argument checks, limits on where data can go, and human approval for risky actions, with a signed record of each decision.

Benchmark · Saaf vs Microsoft Presidio

Fast enough to stay on. Measured in public.

44×faster per kilobyte than Presidio on the same machine
12 mscold start, against more than a second
0.1 vs 691 MBinstalled size, Saaf vs Presidio + spaCy
0.711 vs 0.437strict F1 against Presidio, tuned and with all recognizers on

On our open IndicPII-Bench v1. Most of the corpus is synthetic and Saaf's rules were tuned on the same families, so read the accuracy numbers as optimistic until the held-out human-labelled set is out. Speed and size are measured, not modelled.

Principles

How we build.

Fast by default

Safety that adds latency gets turned off. We budget every millisecond and publish it.

Open source

Code, benchmarks and data are public under permissive licences.

Measured in public

Every claim ships with a number, a method and a way to reproduce it, including where we lose.

Your infrastructure

Everything runs in your cloud or on-prem. We never see your prompts or your data.
