Saaf Guard

In research

A firewall for agent tool calls. Deterministic policy checked before every call runs: allow-lists per task, argument checks, limits on where data can be sent, and human approval for risky actions, with a signed record of each decision. Detection layered underneath, never instead.

How we label projects

  • Live: released, tested in CI, and safe to try on your own data.
  • Beta: usable, but interfaces may change between versions.
  • In research: an idea we are testing. We publish the thesis and reproducible numbers before we call it a product.
