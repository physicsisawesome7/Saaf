Selected work

Products shipped for India's builders.

Automated DPR platform

Assembles Detailed Project Reports for railways and highways from survey, alignment, design and cost inputs, in authority formats (MoRTH, IRC, railway norms). Months of manual work become a largely automated workflow.

DPR validation

An AI and rule-based engine that checks submitted DPRs for code compliance, drawing–quantity–estimate consistency, rate deviations and missing sections, for government scrutiny cells.

Bridge GADs in minutes

General Arrangement Drawings, quantities, BOQ and cost estimates generated from design parameters. Drafting and estimation turnaround cut from days to minutes in pilots.

NHAI drone monitoring

Contributed to the bid and solution design for NHAI's Drone Analytics & Monitoring System: chainage-wise progress analytics and dashboards for highway projects.

NTPC, IOCL and NMCG

Drone surveys and AI analytics for power-plant assets, an AI surveillance architecture for oil and gas installations, and drone mapping of the Ganga under Namami Gange.

Digital twins

Twins built from drone, 360° and site data to compare planned and actual progress and log inspection findings for infrastructure owners.
