Why Smriti

Built for documents that change, and people who see different things.

Answers you can check

Every sentence is matched against the passage it cites, numbers included. If the model's answer doesn't hold up, Smriti shows a verified extract instead, or says plainly what is missing.

Access that holds

Permissions are enforced twice: once during search and again against the source of truth before anything is shown. Personal data is masked by role. Administrators configure the system without reading its content.

Always the version in force

Smriti tracks which document supersedes or amends which. Ask "as of" any date and the answer leads with the text that applied then, never quoting a superseded rule as current.

Platform

From a question to work that gets done.

Agents

Saved assistants with their own instructions, sources and tools: cited lookup, a tool-using loop over search, APIs, MCP servers, SQL and sandboxed Python, or research that splits a question into verified parts.

Workflows

Chain agents, conditions, variables, code, loops and human sign-off on a visual canvas. Every step keeps the same guarantees, and an approval pauses the whole run.

Tools & integrations

Connect REST APIs, MCP servers and databases. Put an agent in your intranet with one script tag, or in Slack, Telegram and Discord, with personal data masked on the way out.

Ingest & watch

Crawl websites, read uploads, folders, feeds, APIs, databases and MCP resources. Smriti notices when a source changes, writes a "what changed" note, and lists every document that cites the changed clause.

Deployment

On your hardware, with models you choose.

One command

An installer detects the GPU and starts the whole stack with Docker: model server, embeddings, reranker, database, vector store, API and crawler.

One consumer GPU

Profiles for 6, 12, 16 and 24 GB cards, or CPU only. Lookups and table questions are answered without calling the model at all.

Open weights

Runs open-weight models you host. No document, question or answer has to leave your network.

Audited

Every answer, tool call, approval and access decision goes into a hash-chained audit log.

Try it in five minutes

Clone the repository, run the installer, and ask the seeded demo tenant a question as different people to watch access, masking and versions at work. Every document in the demo is synthetic.
