Smriti now does workflows and lives where your team works

Lalit Shukla
2 Nov 2026 · Release
ReleaseWorkflowsIntegrations

Two big additions to Smriti. Workflows turn a process into a graph of steps you can draw. Integrations put any published agent in a web page, Slack, Telegram or Discord. Both keep the rules that make Smriti's answers worth trusting.

Workflows

A workflow is a new kind of agent, built on a canvas. It has nine step types: start, AI agent, if / else, set state, sandboxed code, human approval, loop, end and note. Steps share variables, and prompts and outputs are templates such as {{ draft }}.

Conditions use a small expression language that Smriti parses itself, with no eval anywhere, and unsafe expressions are rejected when you save. Three templates ship with it: sign-off before release, answering a list of questions, and routing by topic. See more examples.

Integrations

  • Website widget. One script tag. It renders in a shadow root, streams cited answers and runs only on the sites you allow.
  • Slack. Mention the app or message it; answers arrive in the thread.
  • Telegram. A bot for field staff, with one conversation per chat.
  • Discord. An app with an /ask command.

Each one is created from Agents → Integrations, with setup steps and test buttons built in. See all applications.

What keeps it safe

  • A stated identity. Every integration answers as its creator, as the signed-in user of your site, or as chat users an administrator has mapped. Unmapped people are refused.
  • Masked on the way out. Replies to chat apps have personal data masked by default, and answers citing confidential documents become a link back to Smriti.
  • One approval rule. A workflow pauses for a human step or for any tool action that needs approval, and only the person who asked can approve, in the app or with a chat button.
  • Bounded runs. A workflow stops after 50 steps, and a failure names the step that failed.
  • Verified requests. Slack signatures, Telegram secret tokens, Discord Ed25519 signatures and a widget origin allow-list.

Try it

Pull the latest code and re-run the installer, or follow the quickstart. The new reference pages are Workflows and Integrations.
