Focus areas

Three problems we think India has to solve first.

Privacy for LLMs

Personal data leaks into prompts, logs and retrieval stores. We mask it before it leaves your network and restore it in the reply.

Agent security

Agents act through tool calls, and that is where data walks out. We are researching guardrails that check every call before it runs.

Evaluations for India

Most safety benchmarks are English-only. We build open benchmarks in Hinglish and Indian scripts and publish every number, including the ones we lose.

Benchmark · IndicPII-Bench v1

Measured against Microsoft Presidio, in public.

0.711 vs 0.437strict F1, Saaf vs Presidio with its India recognizers on and tuned
0.35 vs 131false alarms per 1k tokens on look-alike numbers
44×faster per kilobyte on the same machine
0.1 vs 691 MBinstalled size, Saaf vs Presidio + spaCy

Most of the corpus is synthetic and Saaf's rules were tuned on the same families, so read Saaf's scores as optimistic until our human-labelled set is out. Names in Indian scripts remain unsolved for every tool, including ours.
