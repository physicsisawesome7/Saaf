# Data provenance

Every byte of data IndicPII-Bench uses, where it came from and what we are allowed to do with it. `python -m indicpii_bench fetch` writes the exact URLs and SHA-256 hashes to `bench/data/manifest.json`.

## Rules

- **No real personal data is collected.** No social media, leaked dumps, election affidavits or real ID images.
- **Structured identifiers** (Aadhaar, PAN, GSTIN, mobile, UPI, card, bank account) are generated by our own checksum-valid generators.
- **People are synthetic.** Name *token pools* are drawn from public NER corpora; the 200 most frequent names (public figures, deities) and their tokens are removed, and first and last names are recombined at random.
- **A random Verhoeff-valid 12-digit number may equal someone's real Aadhaar.** Generated numbers are never paired with realistic addresses in published corpora, and every corpus file is labelled synthetic. Get counsel review before publishing at a larger scale.
- **Raw downloads stay in `bench/data/raw/`** (git-ignored). Locally built evaluation slices stay in `bench/corpus/external/` (git-ignored).
